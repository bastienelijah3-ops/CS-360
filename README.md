# CS-360

Briefly summarize the requirements and goals of the app you developed. What user needs was this app designed to address?

This app is a private, offline first weight tracking assistant designed for diverse users and groups. Whether you are looking to establish a daily accountability habit to achieve long term body composition goals, endurance athletes, such as runners and cyclists, who aim to optimize power-to-weight ratios ahead of competition while ensuring they do not drop below safe weight, or strength athletes and bodybuilders who need to monitor strict bulking or cutting phases with precise daily entries to assess metabolic rates and adjust caloric intake thresholds; this is the app for you.

What screens and features were necessary to support user needs and produce a user-centered UI for the app? How did your UI designs keep users in mind? Why were your designs successful?

The most important screens were the log in screen and weight screen, tracking screen. The app is not too complex when your weight is put in and your target weight and you keep track that is the benefit of the application. My UI took a simple appraoch  so that even non tech savy users can benefit from the app.

How did you approach the process of coding your app? What techniques or strategies did you use? How could those techniques or strategies be applied in the future?

Project three taught me how to be more specific when coding for diverse user needs rather than just what I think is best or my vision for the project. Specifically for the weight tracker app, I needed to balance those who may not be heavy into working out like me but also provide features for users who are.

How did you test to ensure your code was functional? Why is this process important, and what did it reveal?

It verified that I met all requirements, met goals, and had an app that users are ready to experience. For my first test I saw errors between fludit of the app and made changes to enhance user experience. 

Consider the full app design and development process from initial planning to finalization. Where did you have to innovate to overcome a challenge?

My biggest challenge was properly connecting user needs, my UI, and the actually code. I was particularly successful in demonstrating having code to add items to the databases, providing simple entry and layout that the user will be able to easily add items to the app. Also my implementation provided a method for retrieving and displaying the SMS notification when an weight reaches a certain level.
